# 📦 SPRINT 5 – Inventory Control System

🎯 **Goal:** Stock integrity & tracking.

---

### Deliverables

#### 1️⃣ Stock Deduction
- Auto deduct on sale
- Insert stock movement record

#### 2️⃣ Manual Adjustment
- Lost
- Damaged
- Correction
- Reason required

#### 3️⃣ Low Stock Alert
- Threshold warning

#### 4️⃣ Optional Tracking
- Location tagging (optional flag)

---

### Definition of Done
- Stock always reconcile with transactions
- Adjustment logged immutably
